Common questions

Is this rate before or after tax?

Your target income is treated as income before personal income tax, and the calculator does not estimate tax. Tax rules differ by country and situation, so check local rules or ask an accountant before finalizing a rate.

What counts as billable time?

Time you can charge a client for, such as project work or consulting calls. Admin, invoicing, finding clients, and learning are real work but usually unbilled, which is why billable time is often well below your total working hours.

Does the rate include platform or payment fees?

No. If a marketplace or payment provider keeps a percentage of what you charge, your rate needs to be higher to take home the same amount. Fees vary by platform and sometimes by contract, so check the platform's own current fee page.

Why is my result higher than an employee hourly wage?

Employees are paid for time off and non-billable hours, and employers cover many business costs. As a freelancer, your billable hours have to fund all of that.

Are my numbers saved or sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in your browser, and RateNest does not send or store the numbers you enter.
