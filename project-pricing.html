A TRANSPARENT FORMULA

See how the quote is built

Estimated hours × hourly rate+Labour buffer + direct expenses
  1. Labour subtotal = estimated hours × selected hourly rate.
  2. Uncertainty allowance = labour subtotal × buffer percentage. It applies to labour only.
  3. Suggested quote = labour subtotal + uncertainty allowance + direct project expenses.
  4. Effective hourly rate at estimated hours = suggested quote ÷ estimated hours. This includes direct expenses, so it is not the same as your labour rate or profit.
  5. Effective hourly rate at actual hours (optional) = the unchanged fixed quote ÷ the hours you actually worked. Enter the final hours after delivery to see how an overrun lowers the rate or a faster finish raises it. This quote-per-hour figure includes direct expenses; it is not profit or take-home pay.

The buffer is a markup allowance, not a true target profit margin. A true profit-margin calculation uses a different formula. This tool does not estimate taxes, market rates, or whether a client will accept the quote.

Common questions

Should I include direct expenses?

Include project-specific costs that you want the client price to cover, such as materials or subcontractors. Avoid counting the same expense twice if your hourly rate already accounts for it.

Is the uncertainty buffer profit margin?

No. This calculator adds a percentage of the labour subtotal. That is a markup-style allowance for uncertain work or revisions, not a true profit margin. A true target margin requires dividing cost by one minus the margin percentage.

What happens to my effective hourly rate if a fixed-price project takes longer?

The agreed project quote stays the same unless you and the client approve a change order. Divide that quote by the actual hours worked to see the quote per hour: more hours lower the rate, while fewer hours raise it. Include meetings and revisions in your time total. This figure includes direct project expenses and is not profit or take-home pay.

Does the quote include tax or platform fees?

No automatic tax or fee is applied. Rules and platform fees vary. Add a known direct project cost only when appropriate, and confirm any required tax or fee treatment for your situation.

Are my inputs saved or sent anywhere?

No. Calculations run in your browser. RateNest does not send or store the values you enter.
